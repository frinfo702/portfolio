A running log of things I notice while studying English. They may help me improve later, and they may be useful to someone working on the same problems.

## Approach

- Make time to engage with English every day
- Keep unfamiliar expressions together with their context
- Measure reading, listening, writing, and speaking separately
- Don't confuse test scores with real-world ability
